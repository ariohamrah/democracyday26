We forecast every congressional race using regularized fundamentals, geographically aligned prior election results, incumbency, and national and local polling. We estimate coefficients and uncertainty with whole-cycle historical holdouts, then simulate shared national and regional errors for chamber control, including Senate holdovers. An inspectable name-only “aura” feature tests rhythm, length, alliteration, letter variety, and pronunciation ease; its ablation shows no improvement. Model probabilities remain separate from organizer-supplied November 1 Kalshi prices when maximizing expected competition points. Limitations include incomplete historical publication timestamps, sparse polling, uncertain independent caucuses, and approximate treatment of same-party contests, ranked choice, and runoffs. Missing prices remain blank.
